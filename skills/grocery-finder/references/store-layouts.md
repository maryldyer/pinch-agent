# Store layout reference

## General principles
Most US grocery stores follow a similar layout:
- **Perimeter:** produce, meat and seafood, dairy, bakery, deli.
- **Center aisles:** pantry staples, canned goods, international, snacks, beverages, cleaning, paper goods.

## Trader Joe's
- Smaller format, fewer aisles, stock rotates seasonally.
- International items are mixed throughout, not a dedicated aisle.
- Nut butters and tahini go in the spreads section near peanut butter.
- Canned fish, beans, grains go in the center aisles.
- Refrigerated items (miso, tofu, kimchi) go on the back wall dairy/refrigerated section.
- Frozen goes in the dedicated frozen aisle(s).
- No dedicated Asian foods aisle, ethnic items are integrated.

## Safeway / Nob Hill / Vons / Albertsons (same banner family)
- Larger format, more organized by category.
- Front aisles (roughly 1 to 3): produce, floral.
- Middle aisles: canned goods, condiments, international foods (usually one dedicated Asian/International aisle).
- Baking aisle: tahini is sometimes here near the nut butters.
- Natural/organic section: often a dedicated section for specialty items (miso, specialty vinegars).
- Nob Hill is Raley's owned, with a similar Safeway style layout.

## Whole Foods
- Two places to check for specialty items: the main aisle AND the specialty/bulk section.
- Tahini and nut butters go in the spreads aisle.
- Miso, tofu, specialty Asian ingredients go in the refrigerated natural foods section or the international aisle.
- Bulk section: grains, nuts, some specialty items.
- "365" is their house brand, usually on the middle shelves.

## Target
- Smaller grocery section, limited specialty items.
- International foods go in a small dedicated section in grocery.
- Nut butters and tahini go with the peanut butter in the pantry section.
- Fish sauce, miso, and specialty items are often not stocked, suggest a grocery store instead.

## Kroger / Ralph's / Fred Meyer (Kroger banner)
- Dedicated International Foods aisle, good for Asian, Latin, and European items.
- Natural/organic section: "Simple Truth" brand items.
- Tahini goes in the natural foods section, the international aisle, or with the nut butters.

## Sprouts
- Health focused, strong natural and specialty selection.
- Tahini and nut butters go in the bulk section or the packaged spreads aisle.
- International ingredients are usually in the natural foods section.
- Good for miso, specialty vinegars, alternative flours.

## H-E-B (Texas)
- Large format, excellent selection.
- Dedicated international aisle plus a "Central Market" section in larger stores.
- Most specialty items are well stocked.

## Publix (Southeast US)
- Clean layout, good selection.
- International aisle near the center of the store.
- Natural/organic section separate from the main aisles.
